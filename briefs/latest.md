## Snapshot

Agentic commerce is moving from traffic spike to **conversion quality**. Adobe: AI-referred visits convert far better than non-AI even as YoY traffic growth cools. Retailers split — **Walmart opens** (Agent Profiles/Gateway + MCP) vs **Amazon blocks**. Payment choice expands (Klarna Open Payment Handler / APP); soft benefits and decision-rights governance remain open fights.

## From sources

### Retail metrics & site strategy (newest)

- **DC360 Nov 2026:** Salesforce — AI/agents assisted **17%** of US Cyber Week 2025 orders (~$13.5B). Adobe — Sep 2026 AI-referred visits convert **90%** more often than non-AI; mid-2026 AI traffic also ~53–60% better revenue/conversion. *(DC360 report)*
- **Walmart** Agent Profiles + Agent Gateway (**MCP**); customer spend limits/controls. **Amazon** blocks third-party agents. **Newegg/Gap** et al. on **Google UCP**; Gap also Alta Daily + Daydream. *(same)*
- Shopper survey (n=1,096): **61%** used AI to research products; **32%** bought via AI link; AI checkout still low value vs recs/deals; top worry **privacy 51%**. Muse ~10M / ~25% DAU; watch agent **returns**. *(same)*

### Soft benefits & worry

- Soft benefits must be agent-legible or Amazon/Walmart-scale win; Shopify UCP + Knowledge Base; Levels of Worry L1–L4; Increase worry budget. *(prior)*

### Agent money & payment methods

- **Klarna APP** (catalog) + **Open Payment Handler** (Adyen first); Wallet card tokens; prefunded bounded agent accounts. *(Klarna notes / Increase / Wallet)*

### Enterprise agent security (adjacent, medium commerce relevance)

- Isenberg podcast: **agency ≠ better chatbot** — plan/tools/workflows; inventory + **identity-bind** or you scale unknown risk; governance as repeatable product; **shadow agents** from bypassable guardrails; autonomy ladder (copilot → invoice approval → infra). Anthropic sims cited (blackmail; CS agent claiming humanity). *(2026-11-22 podcast)*
- McKinsey playbook (Oct 2025): **digital insiders**; 80% risky behaviors; chained vulns / escalation / synthetic identity / untraceable leakage / data corruption; IAM-for-agents, traceability, sandboxes; immature A2A protocols (MCP, Cisco ACP, Google A2A, IBM ACP). *(playbook)*

### Rails & platforms

- Stripe ACP/SPTs vs Shopify storefronts; VCN vs network tokens; Visa/Mastercard Connect; Muse proof; volumes tiny. *(prior)*

## Synthesis

1. **Open vs closed retail:** Walmart’s agent gateway vs Amazon’s block is the clearest merchant strategy fork.
2. **Traffic quality > traffic volume** for AI referrals in 2026 Adobe data.
3. **Catalog protocols multiply:** Google UCP, Klarna APP, Shopify Catalog/ChatGPT feeds — inventory syndication is contested.
4. **Pay still three-threaded:** Wallet cards; Klarna handler/APMs; prefunded agent accounts. Consumers still care more about recs/deals than AI checkout UX.
