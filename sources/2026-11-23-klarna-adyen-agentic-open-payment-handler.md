# Klarna + Adyen Agentic: bringing non-card choices to agent checkout

Hey FinTech Fanatic!

Last week, I wrote about ChatGPT Wallet, where an AI agent completes the purchase with the user’s card. But at a normal checkout, a card is only one of the choices.

Klarna and Adyen are now bringing more of those choices to the agent. Shoppers can pay with Klarna when an AI agent checks out for them through Adyen Agentic, Adyen’s agentic commerce suite.

Klarna is the first payment option beyond cards in Adyen Agentic. The agent shows the same Klarna options the merchant already offers: paying in full, in interest-free instalments or with financing. Shoppers review and confirm every purchase before it goes through.

For merchants, the practical part is this: if they already offer Klarna through Adyen, they can switch it on without any new integration work.

What I find interesting is how it was built. Adyen used Klarna’s open payment handler, a published specification that lets any payment provider add Klarna to AI agent checkout.

To me, that’s the bigger picture. If agents can only pay by card, the choices shoppers make at a normal checkout disappear the moment an agent takes over. An open specification lets those choices come along, through the payment provider the merchant already uses.

Adyen is the first to bring it live. With the specification open to every payment provider, I doubt it will be the last.
